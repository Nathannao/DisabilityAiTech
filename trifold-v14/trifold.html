FROM OUR OWN USE

Less grinding. Less context-maxing. Fewer hallucinations. New hardware: $0. Servers running 24/7: none. Memory in files costs nothing to keep.

The design

Fat library.
Thin client.

One folder of plain files: Markdown notes and a JSON ledger of events, names and facts. New facts are reconciled against old ones: dated, sourced, agreed. Originals stored once.

Where it's going

Your doctor has a scribe.

It holds the visit. You have your memory, and on a hard day, fragments. Your intent, and how you express it, are limited by durable memory.

WHAT N.A.O. NEVER DOES

  • Nothing is recorded without your say.
  • The AI never contacts anyone on its own.
