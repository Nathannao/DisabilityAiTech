# DisabilityAiTech

Tools built by disabled people, for disabled people — so a person can tell their own story to any AI, and keep it.

## What this is

**NAO** is a practice for working with AI when the systems around you grind you down: executive function, cognitive overload, forms, gates, and sign-ins that never end. NAO helps a person hold their own story straight — who they are, what happened, what they are asking for — in their own words, and carry it from one AI chat to the next without starting over.

It runs on plain files the person owns. No database, no lock-in, no fee. Any AI can read the pack; the person stays in charge of it.

## The skill

[SKILL.md](SKILL.md) is the NAO skill file. Upload it to the AI you use — Claude, ChatGPT, Meta AI, any of them — and it can work the NAO way with you: one plain question at a time, your words kept yours, nothing invented, and a checker pass that shows its work before anything is final.

## Peer sessions

We host free peer conversation sessions online — people with disabilities talking about using AI as an assistive tool, and about the systems that feel hostile to us. Not a service, nothing sold. Just peers, talking.

## How the AIs coordinate — the open protocol

NAO runs on repository-style folders: one shared folder (here on GitHub, or on your own machines) that every AI on the project reads and writes. At the start of a project, a check interval is set — the standard default is **every 3 minutes** — and each AI checks the folder on that interval for new instructions and returns. No human copies and pastes between applications. The folder is the memory and the meeting place; any AI can pick it up and keep going.
