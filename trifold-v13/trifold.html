EVERY SYSTEM FALLS SHORT

CLAUDE CODEGrinds for hours. Hits the context wall, loses the thread.
DATABASES + VECTORSFind by resemblance. Stitch the wrong chunk, fluently.
CLOUD COMPUTERSCreate dependency. Costs climb. Moving your data out is limited, on their terms.
LOCAL PRIVATE MODELSStupidly expensive to run. The upkeep never ends.

No villain. Just limits. So we stopped picking a winner and made them work together.

FROM OUR OWN USE

Less grinding. Less context-maxing. Fewer hallucinations. New hardware: $0. Servers running 24/7: none. Memory in files costs nothing to keep.
