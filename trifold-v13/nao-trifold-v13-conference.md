## Check before printing

1. **Session details.** The back says "Open sessions on Dec 1 and Dec 9" with no time or link. Add one, or point the QR at where people sign up.
2. **The three "never does" facts** are stated as facts. Make sure each is true of the system as built today, especially "never contacts anyone on its own."
3. **"Claude Code"** is named in the falls-short panel, as in your original. Other vendors aren't named, and Microsoft's Copilot isn't mentioned. Decide whether you want that on a Microsoft stage.
4. **"From our own use"** claims ($0 hardware, no servers) are yours. Be ready to explain them at the table.
5. **HIPAA.** "Imagine it comes along, and is HIPAA-safe" is a vision. Don't say "HIPAA-compliant" until a qualified person signs off.
6. **QR codes.** Both are reused from v10: contact (back) and "the design and the evidence" (inside right). Scan them from a printed page. The old cover "story in full" code isn't included.
7. **Email mismatch:** `outlook.com` here and in `contact.vcf`, `gmail.com` in the README.

## Banked spec: the public plain-language flyer (not built yet)

A separate document for the person in a clinic lobby, not the conference. From my earlier critique, as requirements:

- **One reader:** someone overloaded, tired of repeating their story, wary of anything online. Possibly older, possibly in distress.
- **One action**, with a name, a place and a time: for example, "Come to one session," with the date and how to join. No more reading required.
- **About a third of the words, in large type,** with no jargon (no orchestrator, ledger, vectors, SQLite).
- **Trust up front:** who is behind it, a real face or name, an organization name, and a short "what it never does" box in plain words.
- **No unsourced numbers.** Stories over statistics.
- **Medical and consent lines handled with care:** if it mentions appointments, say plainly who can see what, and that nothing is recorded without everyone's okay.
- **Reuse the warm art and the first-panel text** ("How many ideas died because the focus broke?"), since that is the strongest part.
- **Test with one real reader** before printing.
